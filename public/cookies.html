Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2025

1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how QuickShare uses cookies and similar technologies (like localStorage) when you visit our website.

Short version: We use almost no cookies. Only the ones needed for the site to work.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They help websites remember your preferences.

We also use localStorage, a similar browser feature that stores data locally without sending it to servers.

3. Cookies We Use

3.1 Strictly Necessary

Name Type Purpose Duration
qs-theme localStorage Remembers your dark/light theme choice Persistent
socket.io Session Maintains real-time connection Session only

3.2 Analytical Cookies

We use none. No Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting.

3.3 Marketing Cookies

We use none. No ads, no retargeting, no social media pixels.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Third-party services we use may set their own cookies:

  • Google Fonts — serves fonts; may log your IP
  • QR Server API — only when you generate a QR code

These are subject to their own privacy policies.

5. Your Choices

You can control cookies and localStorage through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Blocking essential cookies may break functionality (like theme persistence).

6. Do Not Track (DNT)

QuickShare respects DNT signals. Since we don't track users anyway, there's nothing to disable.

7. GDPR / ePrivacy Compliance

Because we only use strictly necessary cookies (theme + session), no consent banner is required under EU law. We do not process personal data for marketing purposes.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy. Changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about cookies? Contact us at privacy@quickshare.app
