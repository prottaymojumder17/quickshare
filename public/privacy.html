Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to QuickShare ("we", "our", "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our file transfer service at quickshare.app ("Service").

Short version: We don't want your data. QuickShare is designed to be anonymous and privacy-first.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Files and Text You Transfer

When you upload files or text, they are stored temporarily on our servers to allow the recipient to download them. Files are:

  • Stored in temporary server memory (RAM)
  • Automatically deleted after 10 minutes
  • Never analyzed, scanned, or shared with third parties
  • Never used for training, marketing, or advertising

2.2 Technical Information

We may collect minimal technical information:

  • IP address — used for rate limiting and abuse prevention only
  • Browser type and version — for compatibility
  • Timestamp — when transfers occur

2.3 What We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Names, emails, or personal identifiers
  • ❌ Account information (we have no accounts)
  • ❌ Payment details
  • ❌ File contents or metadata beyond size/type
  • ❌ Browsing history or behavior tracking

3. How We Use Information

The minimal information we collect is used solely for:

  • Delivering your files to the intended recipient
  • Preventing abuse, spam, and malicious uploads
  • Maintaining service reliability and security
  • Complying with legal obligations

We never sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Retention

Your files and text are retained for a maximum of 10 minutes after upload. After this period:

  • The file is permanently deleted from our servers
  • The access code becomes invalid
  • No backup or copy is retained

Server logs (IP, timestamp) are kept for up to 30 days for security purposes, then automatically purged.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Helmet.js security headers
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • No persistent storage of sensitive data
  • Regular security audits

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. You are responsible for sharing codes only with trusted recipients.

6. Third-Party Services

QuickShare uses minimal third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — for typography (may log your IP)
  • QR Server API — for QR code generation (only when you click "Show QR")

We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any tracking services.

7. Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there's nothing to access, correct, or delete. Files are auto-deleted after 10 minutes regardless of any request.

If you're in the EU (GDPR), UK (DPA), or California (CCPA), you have the right to:

  • Know what data we hold (answer: almost none)
  • Request deletion (files are auto-deleted anyway)
  • Object to processing (we don't process for marketing)
  • Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority

8. Children's Privacy

QuickShare is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used our service, contact us immediately.

9. International Transfers

Our servers may be located in various countries. By using QuickShare, you consent to your files being temporarily transferred to and processed in these locations.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of QuickShare after changes means you accept the new policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at:

Email: privacy@quickshare.app
